FAQ

Key details on Sahana Puttabasavaiah's training, theatre credits, voice work, and casting availability.

What stage, screen, and voice roles is Sahana available for?

Sahana performs lead and character roles across feature films (such as Kittur Rani Chennamma), short films (Maaya), Kannada TV serials (Yuge Yuge Mahadeshwara, Siddhalingeshwara Mahime), and repertory theatre with Rangakirana.

Does Sahana direct stage plays and design lighting?

Yes. With an MPA in Theatre from Bangalore University and conservatory training from Ninasam and Nirdigantha, she directed Chandrashekhara Kambara's Tukrana Kanasu (Yuva Rangotsava 2026) and trained in Lighting Design under Sreekanth Cameo.

Which classical, folk dance, and physical disciplines does she perform?

She holds Junior & Senior Certification in Bharatanatyam from Dr. Gangubai Hangal University (Mysore), trained in Kalari with Vinod Mudedla, and performs Kamsale, Kolata, Dollu Kunita, Patta Kunita, Skit, Mime, and Ranga Sangeetha.

Where has she recorded voice dubbing and narration?

Sahana has dubbed for movies and Kannada television serials at Jangama Studios, Sambhrama Media House, and Krishi Studio, alongside event anchoring and script development.
